# 👹 NagMe

**Other apps remind you. NagMe makes sure you do it.**

NagMe is an Android reminder app built around one simple idea:

**A reminder shouldn't disappear just because you ignored it.**

Instead of showing a notification once and hoping for the best, NagMe can **speak your reminder out loud and keep nagging you until you actually do it.**

---

## 🔔 What makes NagMe different?

Traditional reminder apps are easy to ignore.

NagMe is designed to be persistent.

Create a Nag such as:

> "Take the bins out"

NagMe can speak the reminder, wait for a configurable interval, then nag you again.

And again.

And again.

Until you finally hit **DONE**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🗣️ Spoken reminders

NagMe uses Android text-to-speech to say your reminders out loud.

Your phone doesn't just display:

> Take your medication

It **says it**.

---

### 🔁 Persistent nagging

Choose how often NagMe should repeat the reminder.

For example:

- Every 2 minutes
- Every 10 minutes
- Every 30 minutes
- Every hour

The Nag remains active until it is completed or reaches its configured stopping condition.

---

### 🔢 Maximum Nags

Don't want to be nagged forever?

Set a maximum number of Nags.

For example:

**Nag every 10 minutes, maximum 5 times.**

---

### ⏱️ Maximum Nag duration

Instead of limiting the number of reminders, you can limit how long NagMe should keep trying.

For example:

**Nag every 10 minutes for up to 2 hours.**

---

### 😴 Snooze

Need a temporary break?

Snooze the current Nag and NagMe will come back later.

---

### 🔄 Recurring Nags

NagMe supports recurring reminders including:

- Once
- Daily
- Weekdays
- Weekends
- Weekly
- Selected days

---

### 🔊 Voice selection

Choose from compatible text-to-speech voices available on your Android device.

NagMe can also use a global default voice or a voice selected for an individual Nag.

---

### 📜 History

See what happened to previous Nags, including:

- Completed Nags
- Missed or expired Nags
- How many times you were nagged
- When the Nag was scheduled

---

## 👹 The NagMe philosophy

Most reminder apps answer this question:

**"Did I remind you?"**

NagMe is more interested in:

**"Did you actually do it?"**

That's the difference.

## 🤖 Future plans

Future versions of NagMe are planned to introduce AI-powered personalities and more varied spoken reminders.

Instead of hearing exactly the same sentence every time, NagMe could generate increasingly creative ways of getting your attention.

Potential personalities include:

**☘️ Irish Mammy**  
*"I've asked you three times now. Are those bins going to take themselves out?"*

**🪖 Drill Sergeant**  
*"MOVE! Those bins should have been outside five minutes ago!"*

**🇦🇺 Aussie Mate**  
*"Mate... seriously. The bins. Get on with it."*

**🎩 Posh Butler**  
*"If I may be so bold, sir, the bins remain conspicuously indoors."*

The goal is to make reminders more noticeable, personal and difficult to tune out.
