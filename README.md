# 👹 NagMe

**Other apps remind you. NagMe makes sure you do it.**

🌐 **Website:** https://getnagme.com

NagMe is an Android reminder app built around one simple idea:

**A reminder shouldn't disappear just because you ignored it.**

Instead of showing a notification once and hoping for the best, NagMe can **speak your reminder out loud, keep nagging you until you actually do it, and use AI to change the wording as the nagging continues.**

---

## 🤖 AI-powered nagging

NagMe doesn't have to repeat exactly the same sentence over and over.

When AI-generated nagging is enabled, NagMe uses **Google Gemini** to generate fresh versions of the reminder as the Nag progresses.

Create a Nag such as:

> "Take the bins out"

The first reminder might be straightforward.

If you keep ignoring it, NagMe can become increasingly persistent while still keeping the original task and context intact.

The result is a reminder that feels much less like a repeating alarm and much more like someone who actually wants you to get the thing done.

AI is optional. If AI is disabled — or the cloud service is unavailable — NagMe can still fall back to the original reminder and its normal local Android text-to-speech behaviour.

---

## 🎭 AI personalities

AI-generated Nags can have personality.

NagMe supports different nagging styles so the same reminder can sound completely different depending on who you want doing the nagging.

Examples include:

### ☘️ Irish Mammy

> "I've asked you three times now. Are those bins going to take themselves out?"

### 🪖 Drill Sergeant

> "MOVE! Those bins should have been outside five minutes ago!"

### 🇦🇺 Aussie Mate

> "Mate... seriously. The bins. Get on with it."

### 🎩 Posh Butler

> "If I may be so bold, sir, the bins remain conspicuously indoors."

The personality affects the **wording generated by AI**, while Android text-to-speech handles speaking the generated Nag aloud.

## 🔔 What makes NagMe different?

Traditional reminder apps are easy to ignore.

NagMe is designed around **persistence + voice + AI**.

Create a Nag.

NagMe speaks it.

Ignore it?

NagMe comes back.

Ignore it again?

It comes back again — and with AI enabled, it can **change what it says instead of mindlessly repeating the same sentence**.

It keeps going according to the rules you configured until you finally hit:

**DONE.**

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI-generated reminders

Enable AI for a Nag and NagMe can generate fresh, increasingly persistent wording as the nagging continues.

AI generation is performed securely through the NagMe cloud backend rather than exposing AI credentials inside the Android application.

---

### 🎭 Nagging personalities

Choose how you want NagMe to nag you.

Different personalities change the style and tone of the AI-generated reminder while preserving the actual task you're supposed to complete.

---

### 🗣️ Spoken reminders

NagMe uses Android text-to-speech to say reminders out loud.

Your phone doesn't just display:

> Take your medication

It **says it**.

When AI is enabled, the generated Nag is spoken using the same Android speech system.

---

### 🔁 Persistent nagging

Choose how often NagMe should repeat the reminder.

For example:

- Every 2 minutes
- Every 10 minutes
- Every 30 minutes
- Every hour

The Nag remains active until it is completed or reaches its configured stopping condition.

---

### 🔢 Maximum Nags

Don't want to be nagged forever?

Set a maximum number of Nags.

For example:

**Nag every 10 minutes, maximum 5 times.**

---

### ⏱️ Maximum Nag duration

Instead of limiting the number of reminders, you can limit how long NagMe should keep trying.

For example:

**Nag every 10 minutes for up to 2 hours.**

---

### 😴 Snooze

Need a temporary break?

Snooze the current Nag and NagMe will come back later.

---

### 🔄 Recurring Nags

NagMe supports recurring reminders including:

- Once
- Daily
- Weekdays
- Weekends
- Weekly
- Selected days

Task recurrence and nag repetition are independent — for example, a task can occur every day at 6pm while NagMe nags every 10 minutes until that day's occurrence is completed.

---

### 🔊 Voice selection

Choose from compatible text-to-speech voices available on your Android device.

NagMe can use a global default voice or a voice selected for an individual Nag.

---

### 📜 History

See what happened to previous Nags, including:

- Completed Nags
- Missed or expired Nags
- How many times you were nagged
- When the Nag was scheduled

## 🧠 How the AI works

NagMe keeps the core reminder system local while adding AI through a secured cloud path.

```text
Android NagMe app
        |
        +--> Firebase App Check
        |
        +--> Firebase Anonymous Authentication
        |
        v
Google Cloud Run
        |
        +--> Validate App Check
        +--> Validate Firebase identity
        |
        v
Google Vertex AI / Gemini
        |
        v
Generate next Nag
        |
        v
Android NagMe app
        |
        v
Android Text-to-Speech
        |
        v
🔊 NAG!
```

This architecture deliberately separates **AI text generation** from **speech**.

Gemini decides **what NagMe says**.

Android TTS decides **how it is spoken**.

---

## 🛡️ AI security

NagMe does not put Gemini credentials or backend secrets inside the Android application.

The AI request path uses:

- **Firebase Anonymous Authentication** for a pseudonymous user identity
- **Firebase App Check** to help verify requests originate from the NagMe app
- **Google Play Integrity** for App Check in release builds
- **Google Cloud Run** as the secured backend
- **Vertex AI / Gemini** for AI text generation

Firebase manages the authentication and App Check tokens used by the Android client.

---

## 📴 AI is an enhancement, not a dependency

A core Nag should not stop working just because the internet or AI service isn't available.

NagMe retains the user's original reminder text as a fallback.

Conceptually:

```text
AI available
    ↓
Generate fresh Nag
    ↓
Speak AI Nag

AI unavailable
    ↓
Use original reminder
    ↓
Speak original Nag
```

**Cloud and AI enhance NagMe; they do not replace the core reminder engine.**

## 👹 The NagMe philosophy

Most reminder apps answer this question:

**"Did I remind you?"**

NagMe is more interested in:

**"Did you actually do it?"**

That's the difference.

AI makes that idea even more powerful: NagMe doesn't simply repeat itself — it can **adapt the nagging while the task remains unfinished.**

## 🔐 Local-first design

NagMe's core reminder data and scheduling remain local to the Android device.

Cloud infrastructure is used where required for secure AI generation, while the underlying reminder, scheduling and speech system continues to operate locally.

This keeps NagMe's most important rule intact:

**Your reminder should still work even when AI doesn't.**
